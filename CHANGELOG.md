## 2.3
- Added Containers section on the Gear tab, just above Gear, with each bag's contents listed under it. (Can be turned off per client)
- You can now drag items onto a bag to store them. You can also drag them anywhere else on the Gear tab to take them out. They light up dynamically to show you where you can and can't drop.
- Moved the capacity bar shown next to the bag's weight to the data section to prevent truncating the bar.
- Added the right-click option "Move to Another Container".
- Setting "Show Container Move Notifications" (world, off by default).
- Upgrade weight counts on any item it's installed in, not just weapons.
- Lax clone now uses the longest match.
- Bunch of bug fixes.
